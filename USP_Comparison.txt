COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS & USP (Why Us?)
==================================

User Question: "Why use this when Zapier & HubSpot exist?"
Answer: We are the "Apple" approach (Simple) vs their "Lego" approach (Complex).

1. VS ZAPIER (The "Connector")
------------------------------
*   **The Problem with Zapier:**
    *   Zapier is just a "Pipe". It moves data from A to B. It doesn't STORE leads.
    *   To use Zapier, you still need a CRM (Google Sheets, HubSpot, etc.).
    *   **Cost:** Zapier ($30/mo) + CRM ($50/mo) + WhatsApp API ($50/mo) = Expensive stack.
    *   **Complexity:** Setting up a Zap requires technical skill ("If this, then that").
*   **Our Advantage:**
    *   **All-in-One:** We Capture + Store + Analyze + Automate. No extra tools needed.
    *   **Zero Setup:** User just clicks "Connect Facebook". No building workflows.

2. VS HUBSPOT (The "Giant")
---------------------------
*   **The Problem with HubSpot:**
    *   **Overkill:** HubSpot is built for massive enterprises. It has 1,000 features a small gym owner or dentist doesn't need.
    *   **Learning Curve:** You need a certification just to use it properly.
    *   **Cost:** Starts free, but reliable automation quickly jumps to $800/month.
*   **Our Advantage:**
    *   **Niche Focus:** We do ONE thing perfectly: Facebook Leads -> Instant Response.
    *   **Simplicity:** A non-tech business owner can understand our dashboard in 10 seconds.

3. THE "SWEET SPOT" (Our Market)
--------------------------------
We are targeting the "underserved middle":
*   **Freelancers, Real Estate Agents, Gyms, Dentists, Coaching Institutes.**
*   They find Zapier too "techy".
*   They find HubSpot too "heavy/expensive".
*   **They want:** "Just give me my leads and send them a WhatsApp message automatically."

SUMMARY PITCH:
"Zapier joins tools together. HubSpot runs giant companies.
We simply help small businesses close their Facebook leads instantly."
